# Rules for cut

- 2026-09-28: Music is never shorter than the video. (source: Marco, 28 Sep 2026)
- 2026-11-05: No running captions; single keywords and numbers, large, mostly while the speaker is on screen. (source: Marco, 5 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-05: No italic serif type; emphasis by colour and weight. (source: Marco, 5 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-05: No small text in full-screen scenes; only the most important things. (source: Marco, 5 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-05: Cut stutters out. (source: Marco, 5 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-05: Inserts are animated and have a quiet sound effect, not too much. (source: Marco, 5 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: No doubled word at a cut. (source: Marco, a doubled word at 2:04)
- 2026-11-06: The same tone over every cut; if a sentence runs over a take change and sounds different, take it from one take. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: No back of the head and no turned-away head right before or after a cut. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: Show the speaker longer at the start and the end, in between only briefly where he looks into the camera. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: The brand logo top right in every full-screen graphic. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: The end card starts with the sentence about how it was set up. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-06: Every episode gets its own measured colour grade; never copy grade values from an old episode. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-07: The speaker is never on screen for less than 2.5 s between two full-screen parts; hard cuts after clips and 3D. (source: Marco, "not for less than a second")
- 2026-11-07: A full-screen graphic builds up in its first second and does not wait still for its keyword; background and text stay calm. (source: Marco, 7 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-07: Never zoom into third-party clips. (source: Marco, "it looks cheap")
- 2026-11-09: Web pages, tools and references as clean full-screen b-roll, not in a frame on a card; a page that is praised runs all the way through. (source: Marco, 9 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-12: Nothing is shown before both checks show 0 errors; the shown file is locked and every change is a new version. (source: Marco, 12 Nov 2026)
