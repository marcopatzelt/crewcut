# Rules for edit

- 2026-11-10: The mix of 2D and 3D inserts in the first clay-look short is the bar for every new video. (source: Marco, 10 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-10: This way of building (one plan, one command) replaces the old editing workflow. (source: Marco, 10 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: Symbol before text: at most 3 text cards per video and at most a quarter of the inserts as text. (source: Marco, "still feels like PowerPoint")
- 2026-11-11: Symbolic scenes can be drawn 2D scenes as well as 3D; one word or number per scene, on the spoken word. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: The bar is high-quality motion design, not "it renders"; rented GPUs are there for quality. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: Show real material without being asked: the post, the announcement, the third-party result, always with its source. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: Music is made for each episode and follows the story; instruments only, quiet under the voice. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: Several rented GPUs are fine when it is faster; test every new machine first and leave none idle. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: Show the list of inserts (time, kind, one line) before the big render, then keep building. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-12: Recordings show a place or a result with a calm camera, not someone walking. (source: Marco, "the walking in was unnecessary")
- 2026-11-12: Music at -13.5 dB without ducking was a bit too loud; use about -15 dB with ducking. (source: Marco, 12 Nov 2026)
