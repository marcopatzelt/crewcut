# Rules for 3d

- 2026-11-06: Not everything in 3D is in the brand blue; use real materials. (source: Marco, 6 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-10: The 3D look is a light clay look (inspired by the channel fern). (source: Marco, 10 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-10: Final 3D renders run on rented GPUs, not on the own computer. (source: Marco, 10 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-10: Ask before every new render job that costs money. (source: Marco, 10 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-11: A scene has action from the first frame, a large object and small shapes at several depths; never "too empty". (source: Marco, "too empty")
- 2026-11-11: Figures built from basic shapes look cheap; use objects with an action, or a real model. (source: Marco, 11 Nov 2026)
