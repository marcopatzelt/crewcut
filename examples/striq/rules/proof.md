# Rules for proof

- 2026-11-04: Test only what the tool is built for. (source: Marco, 4 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-04: If the tool does not keep its promise, say so honestly and change the title. (source: Marco, 4 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-07: Tests are visible and side by side, plus one test that gives a number. (source: Marco, 7 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-07: Runs go through Marco's subscription, not through paid API keys. Nothing costs money without an OK. (source: Marco, 7 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-07: Do not benchmark other vendors' models at high cost; use published evaluations and fill gaps with the cheap model. (source: Marco, 7 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-08: A test with several tracks uses the same task, the same fresh copy and the same measurement for every track. What is left out of one track is left out of all. (source: studio, 8 Nov 2026)
- 2026-11-08: Test pages load from a local web server, never as file:// pages. (source: studio, 8 Nov 2026)
