# Pictures on the landing page

**Wallpapers** (`wallpapers/`): five landscapes rendered for Crewcut on 16 November 2026 in Blender 5.0.1 Cycles on one
rented RTX 4090, 5120 x 2880, scaled to 2560 and 1280 px wide. Terrain, haze, valley fog and river are procedural,
written for this project. All third-party assets are CC0 (public domain); no attribution is required, we give it anyway:

| Wallpaper | Sky (HDRI, Poly Haven, CC0) | Authors |
|---|---|---|
| `meadow-day` | [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky) | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest |
| `green-dusk` | [Qwantani Dusk 2 (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky) | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest |
| `mint-dawn` | [Kloofendal Misty Morning (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_misty_morning_puresky) | Greg Zaal |
| `golden-hills` | [Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky) | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest |
| `rhine-evening` | [Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/belfast_sunset_puresky) | Dimitrios Savva, Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest |

Trees: [Island Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01) (Poly Haven, CC0), Rico Cilliers, Rob Tuytel.
Ground: [Grass004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004) (ambientCG, CC0).

**Frames** (`frames/`): stills from the author's own STRIQ videos (talking head, gameplay made by Claude, the 3D
scenes of a commercial, a product render), cut so that no words show. They are part of this repository like the
mascot Slate.

**The app window** comes from `docs/readme/` (rendered in CI from the app in demo mode) and is cut to the window by
`site/build.sh`.
