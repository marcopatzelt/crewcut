## Optimize titles before recommending them

For Shorts and long videos, aim for the highest score the evidence supports. Score five distinct drafts, revise them against the findings, and score them again under the same rules. Keep the strongest accurate version of each angle. Show the baseline, best achieved score and remaining deductions on the title card. Two unsuccessful revision rounds establish a best observed score. Call it the maximum only when the reachable ceiling is justified.
