# Rules for upload

- 2026-11-16: Publishing needs a publish card every time. A yes for one video never carries over. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: The upload goes through the YouTube API, never through clicks in a browser. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: The package check shows 0 errors before the publish card. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: The made-for-kids and altered-content answers are shown with a reason on the publish card. (source: Crewcut default)
