# Rules for cut

- 2026-11-16: No word rest and no doubled word at a cut. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: The last complete take of a sentence wins, unless the speaker said otherwise while recording. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: Cuts sit in pauses, never inside a word. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: A full-screen graphic never stands still waiting for its keyword. (source: Crewcut default)
- 2026-11-16: Nothing goes to the user before both checks show 0 errors. (source: Crewcut default)
