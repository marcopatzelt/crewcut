# Read by: python3 skills/script/scripts/script_check.py SCRIPT.md --short
# Format: one phrase per line, case does not matter. A trailing * matches any word ending.
# A line starting with re: is a regular expression (Python syntax, case-insensitive).
# Source: the reel rules in Crewcut's Shorts plan ("not X. not Y. but Z.", "imagine", "game changer", dashes).

# "Not X. Not Y. But Z." and its relatives
re:\bnot\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?[.,;]\s*not\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?[.,;]\s*(?:but|just)\b
re:\bnot\s+(?:just|only|merely)\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?,?\s+but\b
re:\b(?:it'?s|it is|this is|that'?s|that is)\s+not\s+[^.!?]{1,40}?[,.;]\s*(?:it'?s|it is|this is|that'?s)\b
re:\bnot\s+(?:\w+\s+){0,3}\w+,\s+but\s+
