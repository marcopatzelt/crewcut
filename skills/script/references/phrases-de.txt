# Format: one phrase per line, case does not matter. A trailing * matches any word ending.
# A line starting with re: is a regular expression (Python syntax, case-insensitive).
# Source: the reel rules in Crewcut's Shorts plan ("Nicht X. Nicht Y. Sondern Z.", "Stell dir vor", "Game-Changer",

# "Nicht X. Nicht Y. Sondern Z." and its relatives
re:\bnicht\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?[.,;]\s*nicht\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?[.,;]\s*(?:sondern|nur)\b
re:\bnicht\s+(?:nur|bloß|einfach)\s+[^.!?]{1,60}?,?\s+sondern\b
re:\b(?:das ist|es ist|dies ist)\s+(?:nicht|kein|keine)\b[^.!?]{1,40}?[,.;]\s*(?:sondern|das ist|es ist)\b
re:\bnicht\s+(?:\w+\s+){0,3}\w+,\s+sondern\s+
